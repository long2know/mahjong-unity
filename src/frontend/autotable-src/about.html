Mahjong Web

A browser-native WebGL2 mahjong table, with the established lobby, chat, multiplayer client, Changsha server rules and four collaborative tabletop variants.

Choose perspective or flat view, a classic or rectangular board, and an in-table or separate local hand in Display settings. Automatic layout fits narrow screens.

The renderer runs directly in the browser. No game-engine player, Editor, WebAssembly player or external Autotable installation/service is required. Unsupported WebGL2 or a lost context is reported explicitly.

Source and notices
